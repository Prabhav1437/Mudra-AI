Problem Statement

Communication barriers exist between ISL users and people who communicate through spoken languages such as English and Hindi. This creates difficulties in accessing information, education, public announcements, and everyday communication.

Proposed Solution

provide real-time, bidirectional translation between spoken languages and Indian Sign Language.

Audio to ISL: Convert English/Hindi speech into text, interpret its meaning, and generate corresponding ISL signs using animations.

ISL to Speech: Use a camera to recognize sign language gestures and translate them into text and spoken language.

Real-time accessibility: Support applications such as railway announcements, educational content, and everyday conversations.
